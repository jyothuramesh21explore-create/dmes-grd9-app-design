STAGE 4: EXPLICIT INSTRUCTION - Teach Time

⏱ 10:00 (Includes I Do)

The Core Definition

A computer is a machine that works with information.

Computers are designed to help people with thinking work, as opposed to physical work (like a hammer or a bicycle).
