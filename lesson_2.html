Scenario: Imagine that you are going to recommend a pet to someone.

STAGE 2: LEARNING OBJECTIVES & SUCCESS CRITERIA

Learning Objective: We will distinguish between inputs and outputs in mobile applications.
  • I can identify the information a computer gets from users (input).
  • I can identify the information a computer gives back (output).

STAGE 3: DO NOW! - Odd-One-Out

⏱ 4:00

Look at these three vocabulary words: perseverance, improve, refine.

STAGE 4: EXPLICIT INSTRUCTION - Data Flow

⏱ 10:00 (Includes I Do)

INPUT IN

Information computers get from users, devices, or other computers.

➔

OUTPUT OUT

Information computers give to users, devices, or other computers.

Apps solve problems by taking specific inputs and generating helpful outputs.

STAGE 5: TEACHER MODEL - I Do (Pet Chooser App)

Pet Chooser
🐢

"When I click the button that says I am allergic to fur, I am providing an Input."
