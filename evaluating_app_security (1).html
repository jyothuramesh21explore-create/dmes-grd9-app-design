Threat Modeling in App Design

What makes an app secure? As developers, we must evaluate two critical factors:

  • The Threat: A potential negative action or event (e.g., SQL injection, weak passwords, data theft).
  • The Likelihood: How often or how easily that threat might occur based on the app's design.
⚠️ Misconception Alert!

Many people think: "If it's in the Apple App Store or Google Play, it is 100% trustworthy."

Reality: While screened for major viruses, many apps legally track excessive data (like location and contacts). This creates a privacy threat with a high likelihood of occurring if users blindly accept all permissions.
